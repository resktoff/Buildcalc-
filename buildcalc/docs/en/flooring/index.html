What it is for and how to use it

This calculator estimates total square footage or square meters, individual tile/plank counts, and full boxes needed for floor or wall installations.

How to use it step by step

  1. Choose whether to enter room length and width or total area directly.
  2. Set your waste percentage (10% for straight lay, 15% for diagonal patterns).
  3. Enter the tile or plank dimensions (e.g., 60 × 60 cm or 12 × 24 in).
  4. Select whether to calculate boxes by pieces per box or by area per box.

Formula and calculation criteria

  • Net area = Length × Width
  • Total area = Net area × (1 + Waste / 100)
  • Pieces = ceil(Total area / Piece_area)
  • Boxes = ceil(Pieces / Pieces_per_box) or ceil(Total area / Area_per_box)

Net area is multiplied by the waste factor to cover edge cuts, doorways and accidental breakage.

When tile dimensions are entered, single-piece area is computed and total area is divided by piece area, rounded up.

In pieces-per-box mode, total pieces are divided by pieces per box and rounded up.

In area-per-box mode (common for laminate and vinyl), total area is divided directly by box coverage.

How to interpret the results

  • Net area: exact room footprint before waste.
  • Total area (with waste): the area you should purchase.
  • Tiles / planks needed: whole number of individual pieces.
  • Boxes needed: full boxes rounded up so all material comes from the same dye lot.

Practical examples

5 × 4 m room with 60 × 60 cm porcelain tiles

Straight lay with 10% waste and 4 tiles per box.

20 m² × 1.10 = 22.00 m² ÷ 0.36 m²/tile = 62 tiles ÷ 4 = 15.5

Result: 22.00 m² total, 62 tiles and 16 boxes.

12 × 10 ft room with 12 × 24 in planks

10% waste, straight lay.

120 ft² × 1.10 = 132 ft² ÷ 2.00 ft²/plank = 66 planks

Result: 132 ft² total and 66 planks.

200 ft² room using 'By area covered per box'

Direct area mode with 10% waste and 20 ft² per box.

200 ft² × 1.10 = 220 ft² total ÷ 20 ft²/box = 11

Result: 220 ft² total and 11 boxes.

Frequently asked questions

How much flooring waste should I add?

Add 10% for straight installations in rectangular rooms, and 15% for diagonal layouts, herringbone patterns or rooms with many angles.

Why should I order an extra box from the same batch?

Tile shade and caliber can vary slightly between manufacturing batches. Keeping one extra box ensures matching replacements for future repairs.

Does the calculator subtract grout lines?

Nominal tile sizes already account for standard grout joints, so using nominal dimensions plus 10% waste is accurate.

Does it work for laminate or vinyl plank flooring?

Yes. Use 'By area covered per box' (standard on laminate/SPC packaging) or enter plank dimensions.

What if my room is L-shaped?

Split the room into two rectangles, add their areas together, and enter the sum in 'By total area' mode.

Can I use it for wall tiles, and how many tiles fit per m²?

Yes. Enter wall width × height, or add multiple walls together in 'By total area' mode. As a theoretical reference (excluding grout lines, cuts, and waste), a 45 × 45 cm tile is ~4.94 tiles/m² and a 60 × 60 cm tile is ~2.78 tiles/m².
