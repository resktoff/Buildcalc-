Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

1. Overview

BuildCalc is a static construction calculator website. It requires no registration, has no user accounts, and does not collect personally identifiable information.

2. Browser-side calculations

All measurements, quantities and results entered into the concrete, paint and flooring calculators are processed exclusively in your browser using JavaScript. No calculation data is sent to our servers or third parties.

3. Local preferences (localStorage)

BuildCalc stores only two technical preferences in your browser's localStorage: 'bc.units' (to remember your metric or imperial choice) and 'bc.lang' (to remember your language choice when visiting the site root). You can clear these anytime in your browser settings.

4. Cookies

BuildCalc does not set first-party cookies for its calculators. However, because Google Analytics 4 is active, Google may use third-party analytics cookies or identifiers to distinguish sessions and measure traffic.

5. Web analytics (Google Analytics 4)

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and improve site content and performance. Google may collect usage, browsing, approximate device data, and analytics cookies or identifiers. You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and opt out or limit analytics using Google's opt-out browser add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

6. Advertising

Advertising (such as Google AdSense) is currently disabled. If an ad provider is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain cookies and consent choices.

7. Affiliate links

There are currently no active affiliate links on the site. If sponsored or affiliate links are added in the future, they will be clearly labeled next to the relevant content.

8. Hosting and server logs

This static site is hosted on Render. Like any web host, the hosting infrastructure may temporarily log basic technical request data (such as IP address, timestamp and requested URL) for security and availability.

9. External links

This page may link to external websites (such as the project's public repository). BuildCalc does not control the privacy practices of third-party sites.

10. Children's privacy

BuildCalc is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from any user.

11. Calculation accuracy

All results are estimates for planning purposes. Always verify manufacturer specifications and consult a qualified professional before purchasing materials or building.

12. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, there are no user profiles or personal databases tied to your visit. You can remove local preferences ('bc.units' and 'bc.lang') by clearing site data in your browser.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when new features or services are introduced. The last-updated date is always shown at the top of this page.

14. Contact

If you have questions or want to report an issue, you can open an issue on the public project repository at https://github.com/resktoff/Buildcalc-/issues.
