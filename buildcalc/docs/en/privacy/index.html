Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Data we process

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The values you type are never sent to a server. Your unit-system preference is stored locally on your device (localStorage) and can be removed by clearing site data.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate and anonymously, how the site is used (pages visited, device, country). When active, IP addresses are anonymised. You can block it with Google's official opt-out extension or your browser's blocker.

Advertising and affiliates

In the future the site may display clearly labelled third-party ads or affiliate links. If you buy through an affiliate link, BuildCalc may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Ad providers may use cookies under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
